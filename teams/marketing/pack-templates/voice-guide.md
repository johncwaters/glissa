# Voice Guide

<!-- GLISSA:NEEDS-INPUT: replace everything below with this project's real voice rules, then save. -->

Describe how this brand sounds. Be concrete enough that a writer who has never seen the product can
match it. Useful things to include:

- The voice in one line (for example: plainspoken, calm, never hype).
- 3-5 voice patterns to use, each with a short example.
- Sentence and paragraph rhythm.
- A single litmus test the editor can apply per draft (for example: "Would a longtime customer phrase
  it this way?").

The Editor stage audits every draft against this file, fresh, on every run.
